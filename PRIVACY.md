# Privacy Policy — Tip Out

**Last updated: 16 August 2026**

Tip Out is a tip-out calculator for bartenders, published by Layered
Properties.

## The short version

Tip Out does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. Everything
you enter stays on your device. The app has no servers, no accounts, no
analytics, and no advertising.

## What the app stores

All of the following is stored **only on your device**, in the app's
private storage:

- **Your name and text size preference**, so the app remembers them
  between launches.
- **Your roster** — the names and roles of the people you work with.
- **Your tip log** — a CSV file of the shifts you have chosen to save,
  including the date, tip amounts, net sales, and each person's payout.

None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes all of it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not create an account or ask for an email address.
- It does not connect to the internet. The app makes no network requests
  of any kind.
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting, tracking, or advertising.
- It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or
  microphone.

## Sharing a shift

When you tap **Share**, the app hands a summary and a CSV file to your
device's standard share sheet. You choose what happens next — Messages,
AirDrop, email, saving to Files, or cancelling. Nothing is shared unless
you pick a destination yourself, and the app has no visibility into what
you choose.

When you use **Import Shift Data**, you pick a file with the system file
picker. The app reads only the file you select.

## Children

Tip Out is a workplace tool intended for adults. It does not knowingly
collect any information from anyone, including children.
